What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the forms on this site: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, company name and message.

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to work with us, to provide our services. How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as required for business and tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email cathy@infinitymarketing.ie and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a privacy friendly tool that does not set cookies or identify you, and we will update this page.
