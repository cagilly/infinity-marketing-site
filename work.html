JWL Property Valuations

Positioning · Brand identity · Website · Search · Automation

About the client

JWL provides independent property valuations across Dublin and Leinster, for lenders, solicitors, Revenue and the courts.

The challenge

JWL had no digital footprint. People looking for a valuer couldn't find them online, and the business knew it was missing out on work. Day to day admin was manual, time consuming and easy to get wrong.

What we did

  • Brand positioning and messaging, built around a clear idea: evidence before opinion
  • A new brand identity
  • Website design and copy
  • Professional domain, full search optimisation and Google registration
  • Google Business Profile
  • Online enquiries, and online payment for vouchers
  • Workflow automation to remove manual, error prone tasks

The result

Within a few weeks of launch, JWL was on page one of Google for some of its key searches. Enquiries are up and the phone is ringing more.

Taaffe Sand & Gravel

Messaging · Website · Search and AI search

About the client

Taaffe Sand & Gravel supplies quarried and recycled aggregates, sand, gravel and topsoil from its yard in Naas, delivering across Kildare, Dublin and the Midlands. It also provides site clearance, excavation and plant hire.

The challenge

Taaffe needed a website that explained a wide range of materials and services clearly, and showed up when builders, contractors and homeowners searched for them.

What we did

  • Advice on how to present the business and which services to feature
  • Messaging that speaks to the customers they want
  • Website design and copy
  • Search optimisation and AI search optimisation
  • Online enquiries direct to the team

The result

A clear, bold website that does the explaining for them and is built to be found.
